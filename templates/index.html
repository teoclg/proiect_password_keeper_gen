GDPR Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 10.01.2025

We are committed to protecting your data in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. What We Collect

  • Account Info: Name, email, and credentials.
  • Encrypted Vault Data: Passwords and sensitive information (accessible only to you).
  • Usage Data: Logins, IP addresses, and device details.

2. Why We Collect It

  • To provide and secure our services.
  • To comply with legal requirements.
  • To improve service functionality (using anonymized data where possible).

3. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or transfer your data. Contact us at password_keeper@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

4. Data Security and Retention

  • All vault data is encrypted with a key only you know.
  • Data is stored securely and retained only as long as necessary for our services or legal compliance.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. It may be shared with trusted providers or authorities only when required by law.

6. Complaints

For concerns, contact us or file a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority.

By using Password Keeper, you agree to this policy.
