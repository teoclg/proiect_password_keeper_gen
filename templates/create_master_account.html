Set Up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

To secure your account, enable 2FA by scanning the QR code below with an authenticator app like FreeOTP. Follow these steps:

  1. Download and install the FreeOTP Authenticator app from your device's app store.
  2. Open the FreeOTP app and tap the "+" icon to add a new account.
  3. Scan the QR code below to link your account to the app.

Once scanned, you’ll need to enter a 6-digit code from your authenticator app each time you log in.
